Track Me — Browser Extension

Chrome Extension · Time Tracking

A lightweight browser extension that tracks how much time you spend on each website — no accounts, no sign-up, and nothing ever leaves your device.

JavaScript Chrome API Local Storage Chart.js

Track Me is a lightweight browser extension that quietly tracks how much time you spend on each website — no accounts, no sign-up, and nothing ever leaves your device. It knows the difference between actually using a site and just having it open in a background tab, so your numbers reflect real attention, not idle browser clutter.

A quick click on the toolbar icon shows a live, ticking counter for whatever site you're on right now, while the full dashboard gives you the bigger picture: today, this week, or this month at a glance, your top 5 most-visited sites always pinned to the top, and a trend view for any individual site going back up to a full year. It's built for anyone curious where their screen time actually goes — students, remote workers, or just the mildly Instagram-curious — without the guesswork of manually logging it themselves.
